# Fuzzy GPS Implementation Plan

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:subagent-driven-development (recommended) or superpowers:executing-plans to implement this plan task-by-task. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax for tracking.

**Goal:** The unit navigates on a simulated receiver measurement: truth plus per-satellite range errors solved through
the real geometry, with Doppler velocity, smoothed by a per-axis Kalman filter. Tests stay exact unless they opt in
(#402).

**Architecture:** `GpsSolution.computeGeometry` additionally writes the coefficients that map each measurement row's
error to a north/east position error. `GpsErrorModel` keeps the per-PRN error processes and produces position and
velocity errors. `GpsPositionFilter` smooths them. `GpsMeasurement` is the strategy (`ExactMeasurement`,
`SimulatedMeasurement`) that `GPS.tick` calls. `KLN90BPlatform` creates it: the sim gets the simulated one, tests the
exact one.

**Tech Stack:** TypeScript (es2017), MSFS Avionics Framework (`GeoPoint`, `Vec3Math`, `GPSSatComputer`), Vitest (unit,
render, flight).

**Spec:** `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-11-01-fuzzy-gps-design.md`

## Global Constraints

- Error budget: σ_s = 4.9 m with τ = 300 s (Gauss-Markov), σ_f = 1 m white, σ_d = 0.05 m/s white, σ_altRate = 0.5 m/s
  white; σ_UERE = 5 m and σ_ALT = 30 m stay as in `GpsSolution.ts`.
- Altitude row error = the unit's indicated altitude − `PLANE ALTITUDE`, in meters, no random part; it enters whitened,
  multiplied by `SIGMA_UERE_M / SIGMA_ALT_M`.
- Filter process noise q = (3 m/s²)². Restart thresholds: `dt` > 30 s, or the truth moved more than 1 NM beyond `v·dt`
  in one update.
- Truth SimVars of the simulated strategy: `PLANE LATITUDE`, `PLANE LONGITUDE`, `VELOCITY WORLD Z` (north),
  `VELOCITY WORLD X` (east), both read in `meters per second`, `PLANE ALTITUDE`. Never `GPS GROUND SPEED` or
  `GPS GROUND TRUE TRACK` (the unit writes them). Each SimVar is read once per tick.
- The exact strategy reproduces today's behavior bit for bit and never calls `Math.random`.
- No allocation in the per-tick path. `GPS.coords` is one `GeoPoint` for the life of the unit, updated in place
  (maintainer, 2026-11-01; no reader keeps the object).
- `GpsSolution.ts`, `GpsErrorModel.ts` and `GpsPositionFilter.ts` import nothing from the project (pure modules; no new
  circular dependency).
- Take-home mode never calls the strategy.
- Project rules from `CLAUDE.md`: manual page references in comments, no manual text copied, `target: es2017`, American
  English, no counts in prose for collections that grow.
- Commits on the branch are `references #402 …`. The last task squashes the implementation commits (not the design and
  plan commits) into one `fixes #402 …` commit, after the maintainer approves the sim flights.

## Review Focus

1. **A forced acquisition** (engine-running start, SET 10 FAST): `acceptForcedSolution` calls `gpsAcquired` outside the
   tick. The measurement must start from the truth there, so the first `coords` is finite and the >2NM check compares a
   real position. Test in Task 5.
2. **Sim time going backwards** (a new flight, a time change in the sim): `dt` < 0 must not freeze the outputs forever.
   The strategy restarts and takes the new time as its reference. Test in Task 4.
3. **The antimeridian and high latitudes:** the filter's north/east step must wrap the longitude and not divide by
   zero at the pole. Test in Task 3.
4. **The rows change under the error model:** a satellite leaves, the altitude input disappears (air data failure).
   Stale coefficients beyond the current row count must not be used, and no NaN may appear. Test in Task 2.
5. **A singular or NaN variance** (`cNorth`/`cEast` NaN): the filter must not take it as a measurement noise and poison
   its state for good. It skips the position update and keeps predicting. Test in Task 3.

### Task 2: The error model

**Files:**
- Create: `kln90b/GpsErrorModel.ts`
- Test: `test/unit/GpsErrorModel.test.ts`

**Interfaces:**
- Consumes: `ALTITUDE_WEIGHT`, `MAX_ROWS` (Task 1).
- Produces:
  - Constants `SIGMA_SLOW_M = 4.9`, `TAU_SLOW_S = 300`, `SIGMA_FAST_M = 1`, `SIGMA_DOPPLER_MPS = 0.05`,
    `SIGMA_ALT_RATE_MPS = 0.5`.
  - `class GpsErrorModel`:
    - `constructor(random: () => number = Math.random)`
    - `reset(): void`: forgets every satellite's state.
    - `update(dtS: number, prns: readonly number[], coefficients: Float64Array, altitudeErrorM: number | null, out: Float64Array): void`.
      Writes `out[0..3]` = position error north, east (m), velocity error north, east (m/s). `prns` lists the
      satellite rows in order. When `altitudeErrorM` is not null, the altitude row is row `prns.length`.
  - `slowError(prn: number): number`: the slow part of a PRN's error, for tests.
  - Per-PRN state in a preallocated `Float64Array` indexed by PRN (SDK PRNs stay below 256) plus a "known" flag. A PRN
    that is in `prns` and not known starts with a stationary draw; a known PRN missing from `prns` is forgotten.
- Box-Muller keeps the second value of each pair.

- [ ] **Step 1: Write the failing tests:**
  - `it('the slow error is stationary at SIGMA_SLOW_M')`: one PRN, 20 000 steps of 1 s with a seeded generator: the
    sample σ of `slowError(prn)` is within 5% of 4.9, and the sample σ of `out[0]` (coefficient 1 north) is within 5% of
    5.0, the total with the fast part.
  - `it('the slow error decorrelates over TAU_SLOW_S')`: the lag-300 s autocorrelation is within 0.1 of 1/e.
  - `it('one step of 600 s equals the stationary distribution')`: after a 600 s step the correlation with the old
    value is exp(−2) within 0.05 over many trials.
  - `it('a satellite that leaves and comes back starts fresh')`.
  - `it('the altitude row error enters whitened')`: all satellite coefficients 0, altitude coefficient north 1,
    `altitudeErrorM` 300: `out[0]` = `300 * ALTITUDE_WEIGHT` exactly.
  - `it('ignores coefficients beyond the current rows')` (Review Focus 4): fill `coefficients` with 1e6 beyond row n;
    the output stays below 100 m. Then drop the altitude row: no NaN.
- [ ] **Step 2: Run** `npx vitest run --project unit test/unit/GpsErrorModel.test.ts`. Expected: FAIL (module missing).
- [ ] **Step 3: Implement** `GpsErrorModel` in `kln90b/GpsErrorModel.ts`. Gauss-Markov step:
  `e = a*e + SIGMA_SLOW_M*Math.sqrt(1 - a*a)*n`, with `a = Math.exp(-dtS / TAU_SLOW_S)`. Velocity errors use the same
  coefficients with σ_d per satellite and `SIGMA_ALT_RATE_MPS * ALTITUDE_WEIGHT` for the altitude row.
- [ ] **Step 4: Run** the test and `npx tsc --noEmit`. Expected: PASS, clean.

### Task 3: The filter

**Files:**
- Create: `kln90b/GpsPositionFilter.ts`
- Test: `test/unit/GpsPositionFilter.test.ts`

**Interfaces:**
- Produces:
  - `PROCESS_NOISE = 9` ((3 m/s²)², the spectral density of white acceleration).
  - `class GpsPositionFilter`:
    - `lat: number`, `lon: number` (degrees), `vNorth: number`, `vEast: number` (m/s), `initialized: boolean`.
    - `restart(): void`: `initialized = false`.
    - `update(dtS: number, lat: number, lon: number, vNorth: number, vEast: number, posVarNorth: number, posVarEast: number, velVarNorth: number, velVarEast: number): void`.
      When not initialized it takes the measurement as its state, with the given variances as covariance. Otherwise it
      predicts over `dtS`, then updates position and velocity per axis.
- Per axis a 2×2 covariance written out as three numbers; no matrix code, no allocation. Innovations in meters:
  north = Δlat × R, east = Δlon (wrapped to ±180°) × R × cos(lat), with R = 6378100 m (the SDK's earth radius).
  Moving the state by `v·dt` uses the same flat step.

- [ ] **Step 1: Write the failing tests:**
  - `it('converges to a constant measurement')`.
  - `it('follows a velocity step within one update')`: constant measured position track at 0 then 60 m/s north; after
    one update `vNorth` is within 1 m/s of 60 with velocity variance 0.0025 and position variance 25.
  - `it('turns a 20 m position step into a glide')`: at constant velocity, a 20 m step in the measured position moves
    the estimate by less than 10 m in the first update and by more than 18 m after 30 updates.
  - `it('does not lag a constant-velocity truth')`: 120 kt east for 120 updates with exact measurements: the position
    error stays under 1 m.
  - `it('wraps the antimeridian')` (Review Focus 3): flying east across 180° at 60 m/s, `lon` goes from 179.99… to
    −179.99… and the position error stays under 1 m. Also at 89.9°N no NaN.
  - `it('skips the position update with a NaN variance')` (Review Focus 5): pass NaN position variances for 5 updates:
    the state keeps predicting with the velocity, stays finite, and a later finite measurement is taken normally.
- [ ] **Step 2: Run** `npx vitest run --project unit test/unit/GpsPositionFilter.test.ts`. Expected: FAIL.
- [ ] **Step 3: Implement** `GpsPositionFilter`. Discrete white-acceleration model: Q = q·[[dt³/3, dt²/2],[dt²/2, dt]].
  Sequential scalar updates (position, then velocity) avoid a 2×2 inverse.
- [ ] **Step 4: Run** the test and `npx tsc --noEmit`. Expected: PASS, clean.
- [ ] **Step 5: Commit** `references #402 Doppler-aided GPS position filter`.
